Chapter II

The Invitation

She appeared in the doorway of the library as if the shadows had simply chosen to become a woman.

Isolde. Platinum-blonde hair swept into an elegant updo that somehow still looked soft. Skin so pale it seemed to hold the moonlight. Glowing red eyes that did not blink often enough. Dark burgundy lips. She wore a black lace high-collar Victorian gown, the intricate detailing catching every flicker of candlelight, sheer sleeves revealing the delicate line of her arms.

She held a single dark rose.

“You keep trying to leave,” she said, voice low. “The road has a way of bringing you back.”

Elias did not ask how she knew. Some things are better left unasked in a place like this.
