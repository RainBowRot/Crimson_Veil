Chapter VIII

When Dawn Forgets

He woke because some old part of him still expected light.

The windows remained black. Isolde watched him from the chair, already dressed in the same black lace high-collar gown, red eyes calm. The tattoos across his chest looked darker in this light, as if the night had claimed them too.

“You were waiting for the sun,” she said.

“How long?”

“Long enough that you should have noticed.” She came to him, gloved fingers tracing the ink at his collarbone. “The hotel does not keep hours, Elias. It keeps guests.”

He caught her wrist.

“And if I want to stay past being a guest?”

Her red eyes softened.

“Then you stop asking the dark for permission.”
