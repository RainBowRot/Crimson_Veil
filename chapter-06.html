Chapter VI

What the Night Takes

She undressed him the way one opens a gift that has been waited for. When his shirt fell away the full map of his tattoos was revealed — neck, chest, arms — dark and deliberate against warm skin. He returned the favor, sliding the lace from her shoulders until the gown pooled at her waist.

She leaned in until their foreheads touched.

“Tell me to stop,” she said, “and I will.”

His answer was quiet. “Don’t.”

So she didn’t.

What followed was deliberate. A slow claiming of breath and pulse and willing surrender. She taught him the difference between being taken and being chosen. He taught her the rare heat of a mortal heart that had decided, fully, to stay.
