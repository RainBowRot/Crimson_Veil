Chapter X

One Eternal Night

They did not return to the road.

There was no road left that would have them.

In the time that was no longer measured by sun, Elias learned the quiet work of the hotel. Isolde taught him the difference between hunger and want. He taught her what it felt like to be chosen by someone who could still remember sunlight.

Sometimes they danced in the empty ballroom. Sometimes they stood at the cliff’s edge and let the fog erase the world. Always they came back to each other — her pale hand against the dark ink on his chest, his tattooed fingers carefully cradling the back of her updo.

The Crimson Veil no longer waited for travelers.

It had what it wanted.

And so did they.
