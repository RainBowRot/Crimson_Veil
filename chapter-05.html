Chapter V

The First Surrender

She did not rush him.

Isolde settled across his lap, the intricate black lace of her gown spilling around them both. One of his tattooed hands rested at the small of her back; the other traced the high collar of her dress. The contrast of her porcelain skin against the dark ink on his fingers made him catch his breath.

“You can still walk away,” she whispered against his throat. “The door is open.”

His hands slid into her platinum hair, careful of the updo.

“I know,” he said. “I’m not going to.”
