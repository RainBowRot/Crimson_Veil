Before you enter

Content warnings

This is a gothic dark romance. It ends in belonging. It does not pretend the night is safe.

All characters are adults. This book is not a template for a real relationship. The hotel is a predator. The love story is the argument with that fact.

  • Possessive immortal / morally gray heroine
  • A place that traps guests (isolation, looping corridors, captivity by architecture)
  • Time theft — dawn withheld without full consent, later confronted
  • Blood, vampiric bite, hunger
  • On-page adult sensuality (open door; power-aware; a stopped bite)
  • Implied deaths of prior guests; a ledger of the kept
  • Grief, leaving, and the fear of being filed away

Heat sits in the middle of the book and is meant to change who holds power. The happily-ever-after is earned by treason against the house, not by forgetting what the house did.
